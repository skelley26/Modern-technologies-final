# Final-project-modern-technologies
## Problem
Too many times have I bought fresh produce thinking that this latest health kick will take and I won’t have to toss everything out in 5 weeks. Physically making a static list would work for a week until it gets too tiring to keep track of and I slip into old habits. So, I decided to utilize the glowing rectangle in my pocket that I am addicted to and make a program that is a one stop shop. 
 


 

## Solution 
With this program, you can add an inventory of all the items in your fridge and pantry along with their expiration dates. All organized in an easily identifiable traffic light system where red is very close to expiration, yellow is nearing expiration, and green is fresh. With an at-a-glance view of all the things about to go bad in your fridge, you are also able to search recipes by ingredient, as well as search what items you have in the list. Delete the ingredients as you go, archiving what you’ve used for reference for future shopping trips. For items with no set expiration date, a recommended date will be used, as well as a guide for how to tell when fresh produce is no longer safe for consumption.

 

## Technology Choices:
For the development of the frontend, HTML and CSS will be utilized. For the backend development, Dockerfile and Node.js will be used; especially with the functionality of the APIs I plan to use. If I use a database, MongoDB seems to be the most realistic as it works well with JavaScript, otherwise I am familiar with SQL. The project will be deployed with Google Cloud.
